The shorthand

Light forward

Paper, peach, and apricot carry most of every surface. Coral and garden come next. Wine and dusk are accessories — ink, a rule, one dark object.

One number

Set S, the radius of the spark’s points, and the geometry follows: tile 2S, spacing S ⁄ 3, margins 2S ⁄ 3, clear space S ⁄ 2.

Two faces

Cormorant Garamond for what a guest reads first. Jost for every working word. Nothing else.

Six devices

Tile, quarter, open field, lattice, crop, point. One per surface. Anything past them is decoration.
