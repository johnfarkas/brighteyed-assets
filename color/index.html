Light forward

The café is called Bright Eyed, and the surfaces should say so before a word is read. Paper, peach, and apricot are the staples. Coral and garden come next. Wine and dusk are accessories — they earn their weight by staying rare.

Staples

Paper, peach, apricot. The ground under almost everything: menus, cups, bags, signage fields, pages.

Seconds

Coral and garden. Large enough to be felt, and used once per surface.

Accessories

Wine and dusk. Type, rules, one emphasis block, the occasional dark object. Rarity is what gives them their contrast.

Contrast, measured

Wine reads on every ground in the palette, from paper at 15.9 : 1 down to coral at 5.5 : 1, which makes it the safe default. Paper reverses out of wine and dusk only.
