Logo

A square quartered into four colors with the spark knocked out in paper. Paper cuts the spark out of the color, so the palette carries the identity.

Vertical lockup

The primary. The tile's vertical edges land in the letter gaps — between the b and the r, and between the e and the d — so neither edge crosses a letterform. Use it wherever the space is square or taller.

Horizontal lockup

For a line: fascia signs, letterhead, cup bands, email. Two intervals, both multiples of S ⁄ 3 — the tile takes two units, the word space takes one. Overall ratio 4.59 : 1.

Icon

The tile alone, for favicons, avatars, app icons, and stamps. Coral and garden are grounds, so they take the icon as well as the ink versions do.

Using it

Clear space

Hold S ⁄ 2 outside the tile on all four sides. On the lockups, measure from the tile, and let the b and d overhang as drawn.

Minimum size

The four-color tile holds to 40 px, where each quadrant is still 20 px across. Below that, switch to a single ink, which carries to 24 px.

Single ink

The spark knocks out to transparent, so the ground shows through. Wine reads on every color in the palette. Paper is for wine and dusk grounds only.

Never

Stretch the tile. Rotate the spark off-axis. Put peach or apricot in a quadrant. Set both darks on one side. Lay the mark straight onto a photograph.

On the SVG lockups: the wordmark is live text in Cormorant Garamond SemiBold. Install the font, or use the PNG. An outlined SVG and vector EPS for print vendors are in production.
