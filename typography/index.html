Typography

Cormorant Garamond for what a guest reads first. Jost for every working word. Both are open source under the SIL Open Font License, so they can be installed, embedded, and sent to a vendor at no cost.

Cormorant Garamond

SemiBold 600 and Regular 400. The name, section titles, menu headings, and the one line on a poster. Its x-height is small and its contrast is high, so it stays a display face: 20 px and above on screen, 14 pt and above in print.
