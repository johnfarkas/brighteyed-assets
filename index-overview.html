How the system works

One number governs everything. Set S — the radius of the spark's points — and the rest of the geometry follows.

The tile is 2S

Make the square exactly two spark-radii across and the four points land on the four edges, touching each at its midpoint. The spark describes the square.

S ⁄ 3 is the spacing unit

It governs both lockups, and the page too: gutters, rules, vertical rhythm. Margins are 2S ⁄ 3.

S ⁄ 2 is the clear space

Hold that much on all four sides of the tile, wherever it sits. The tile's color must always be seen to end.

Rotation only

The tile turns 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°, and nothing else. Never re-proportion, never scale unevenly, never rotate off-axis.
